Year 1

Pick a skill, have a go, then use the games to practise again.

What shall we learn?

➕ Number Bonds

Learn pairs that make 10 and 20.

🔤 Phonics

Blend and segment words using familiar sounds.

✍️ Sentences

Use capital letters, finger spaces and full stops.

📏 Measure

Compare length, height, weight and capacity.

🕐 Time

Tell the time to the hour and half hour.

📚 Reading

Answer simple questions about a short text.

⭐ Mini challenge

Choose one activity above. Work for 10 minutes, then explain one thing you learned to a grown-up.
