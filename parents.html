👨‍👩‍👧 For grown-ups

Keep learning light

Short, regular practice is often easier for children than a long worksheet session.

⏱️ Keep it short

Try 10–15 minutes on one skill, followed by a break or game.

💬 Ask them to explain

“How did you work that out?” helps children practise reasoning.

⭐ Praise the process

Notice effort, trying a new method and correcting a mistake.
