🎒 Ages 4–5

Reception

Pick a skill, have a go, then use the games to practise again.

What shall we learn?

🔤 Phonics

Say letter sounds, spot rhyming words and blend simple words.

🔢 Counting

Count objects to 20 and match numbers to groups.

🔺 Shapes

Name common 2D shapes and talk about their features.

📖 Story Time

Retell a familiar story in the right order.

✍️ Early Writing

Practise writing your name and simple CVC words.

🌍 Understanding the World

Talk about seasons, families, animals and the world around us.

⭐ Mini challenge

Choose one activity above. Work for 10 minutes, then explain one thing you learned to a grown-up.
